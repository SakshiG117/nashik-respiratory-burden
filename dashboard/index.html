Research limitations and health-burden status
This prototype displays available air-quality observations, ward population references and 21 reported hospital records. These records represent hospital-disease-period entries, not necessarily unique patients or citywide totals. Hospital-reported cases are not confirmed pollution-attributable cases. Environmental observations and hospital reporting periods do not yet align sufficiently for a defensible local burden estimate. The pollution-attributable respiratory health-burden model remains under development. No unvalidated estimates are presented as actual cases.
